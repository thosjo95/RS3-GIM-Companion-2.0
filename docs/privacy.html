Privacy notice

GIM Companion: the in-game plugin and the website. Last updated: November 2026.

Who is responsible

GIM Companion is run by an independent developer (RuneScape community and GitHub name thosjo95), not by Jagex. The person responsible for your data is the developer. Contact, including every request below: rs3groupiron@pm.me.

What the plugin sends

You choose to connect the plugin. It only sends data about your own character, and never anything about other players.

The plugin does not send chat messages, friends or clan lists, your RuneScape login, password or payment details, or anything from outside the RuneScape client.

What the website collects

  • Public hiscores data of the group's characters (skills, XP, ranks, clue scrolls) and the public Adventurer's Log events, which Jagex publishes for any character.
  • Things your group writes in the dashboard: goals, notes, item requests, competitions, and the group secret (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Technical data: the web server records your IP address, the time and the address requested, to run and protect the service.

Group pages show hiscores data to anyone who opens the group. Everything that comes from the plugin (banks, quests, kills, drops, who is online) is visible only to people who unlocked the group with its secret. The public Global Leaderboard names groups and players only from hiscores and Adventurer's Log data. From the plugin it shows only totals added up over every group (for example how many players are online, or how many boss kills were made this week), never anything about one group or player. Groups set to "unlisted" are never named there.

Why, and the legal basis

  • Providing the service you asked for (showing your group its progress): your consent, given when you connect the plugin and allow its network access. You can withdraw it at any time by removing the plugin or by contacting me.
  • Running and securing the service (access logs, rate limits, backups): legitimate interests, which are keeping the service working and safe.

Data is not sold, used for advertising or profiling, or shared for any other purpose.

Who receives it

  • Hetzner Online GmbH, the hosting provider (server in Helsinki, Finland, in the EU), acts as a processor and stores the data.
  • Discord, only if your group's owner chooses to connect a Discord webhook. Then short event messages (for example "level 99 reached", "goal completed", a drop) are sent to the channel they chose. You can ask your group not to enable this.
  • Nobody else. There are no advertising or analytics services.

Where it is stored, and transfers

The plugin sends data over HTTPS to https://api.groupiron.com, a server in Finland. Your data stays inside the EU/EEA. The only transfer outside it is the optional Discord webhook above, which your group's owner controls.

How it is protected

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • The plugin sends the group secret once to connect and keeps only a key for your character. A key can only send that character's data while the character is a member of the group; only a hash of it is stored, and the group can disconnect it at any time.
  • Group secrets are stored as salted hashes. Attempts to connect with a wrong secret are rate limited.
  • Limits on how often requests are accepted, checks that an update really comes from the character it claims, and strict validation of everything received.
  • The server runs under an unprivileged account with a firewall, automatic security updates and regular backups.
  • Only the developer has administrative access.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to rs3groupiron@pm.me; I answer within one month. For a quick removal, a group member with access can use Manage group on the website to remove you, which deletes your data immediately.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection supervisory authority.
